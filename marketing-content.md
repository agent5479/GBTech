## Crawler-ready websites — what that means and why it pays

Most small-business sites on rented builders are **hard for crawlers to trust**: bloated scripts, thin or duplicated pages, weak local entity signals, and nothing written for AI systems. GBTech builds the opposite: **static, owned HTML that search engines and AI bots can crawl, render, and cite.**

### Business benefit (the “so what”)

| If the site is crawler-ready… | The business gains… |
|-------------------------------|---------------------|
| Google/Bing can index every service page | Local searches surface the right offer (repair, booking, migration), not a vague homepage. |
| Name, phone, address, and services match across site + schema (+ GBP when set up) | Stronger local entity — fewer “wrong business / wrong town” mix-ups. |
| Pages answer questions in plain HTML (not JS-only shells) | Faster indexing; content still readable if a crawler is picky about JavaScript. |
| AI bots are allowed and given a clear `/llms.txt` map | Higher chance assistants recommend the correct services and contact details. |
| Demo/staff tools stay blocked from indexing | Marketing pages stay clean; internal apps do not pollute search results. |
| Client owns the accounts | No platform lock-in if a builder changes pricing or blocks exports. |

### What Warwick actually ships for crawler readiness

Delivered as part of Essential / Advanced (and migrations off Wix/Squarespace) — not a vague “SEO upsell”:

1. **Crawler access** — marketing URLs allowed; staff/demo areas Disallowed. AI **search/user** bots kept open (e.g. OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-SearchBot, Claude-User, PerplexityBot, Perplexity-User) so assistants can cite the site; training tokens (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended) set deliberately per policy, not blocked by accident via CDN/WAF.
2. **Renderable pages** — public marketing routes as **static HTML** (not a blank JS shell). HTTPS, one canonical URL per page, sitemap of indexable URLs only.
3. **Entity clarity** — consistent business name, phone, email, location; structured data (JSON-LD LocalBusiness / Service) so machines know who you are and what you offer.
4. **Service architecture** — one clear URL per high-intent service where it matters; hub → leaf linking so crawlers and humans follow the same map.
5. **AI-readable index** — hand-written `llms.txt`-style guidance (brand one-liner + key pages) so AI systems prefer the right URLs over thin demos.
6. **Local search handover** — Google Business Profile–ready business details aggregated for Search, Bing, and GBP; client briefed to connect Search Console / Bing and point GBP at the canonical homepage after go-live.
7. **Ownership** — code and hosting in accounts the client controls; free permanent hosting on the GBTech package path; domain fee separate.

### What crawler-ready is *not*

- Guaranteed #1 ranking or invented backlinks  
- A library of generic how-to blog posts  
- Promoting demo/sandbox apps as citation sources  
- Inventing social or GBP URLs that do not exist yet
