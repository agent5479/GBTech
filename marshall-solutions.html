How we work

Pay once. Keep changing it. Stop the monthly bill.

Wix and Squarespace bill you every month just to stay online, then bill you again when you want a different layout or an extra widget. A Marshall site is a one-off build in accounts you control. GitHub Pages hosting is included; the domain name is extra. When the hours, phone number, or copy need to change, you are not stuck waiting on a website-builder upgrade.

The money stays here

One setup cost instead of renting someone else’s website forever.

  • $65+ a month to Wix or Squarespace adds up fast — this is a one-off, not a subscription
  • Hosting is included. You only pay the registrar for your domain name, usually yearly
  • You are not forced into Calendly, form plugins, or a “next plan” to keep the doors open
  • Booking software (Apps Script or Firebase) is only on Advanced — you do not pay for tools you will not use

You can still change it

Templated builders make small edits awkward. You own this site, so changes are yours to make.

  • I build it in your accounts and hand you the keys — or I sit with you so you can edit it yourself
  • Hours, phone numbers, photos, and pages can change without buying a new theme or plugin
  • If you want to run it, I set up Cursor with you and show you how to ship a change
  • Support stays available when you want a hand. You are never locked out of your own site
