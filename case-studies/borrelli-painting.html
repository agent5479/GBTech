Borrelli Painting — website for a Golden Bay trades business

Gianluca (Luca) Borrelli provides interior and exterior painting services across Golden Bay. GBTech engineered a clean, owned digital presence on borrellipainting.nz with prerendered SEO and project showcase galleries — zero monthly builder fees.

The Challenge

Local trades reputation without recurring SaaS overhead

Quality trade businesses thrive on local word-of-mouth, but potential clients searching for painters in Tākaka and Golden Bay need immediate visual proof of workmanship and straightforward ways to get in touch. Renting a bloated site builder meant paying monthly fees indefinitely for static marketing pages.

What We Built

Owned, prerendered web presence

  • Custom owned website live on borrellipainting.nz
  • Prerendered HTML ensuring fast mobile loading and instant indexing by Google, Bing, and AI search engines
  • Categorized project galleries (interiors, exteriors, on-the-job) highlighting craftsmanship
  • Direct click-to-connect channels for rapid phone and email inquiries
  • Zero monthly builder rent — the client owns the code and deployment permanently
