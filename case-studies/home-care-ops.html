Home-Care Operations System

An independent care team needed one system for multiple elderly and special-needs clients — not a tangle of spreadsheets, group chats, and paper notes. The simulated visit and rounds apps below show the same staff + management split.

Problem

Coordination without a shared source of truth

Client details, recurring care tasks, and staff availability lived in different places. Hand-offs between carers risked missed visits and incomplete notes. The team needed mobile access that matched how care actually runs day to day.

Client identity is withheld; outcomes and workflow are first-party. Try the sim: Care Visit · Round Board.

What we built

Care workflow in accounts they own

  • Client management and care-plan style records for multiple households
  • Staff coordination and scheduling for recurring care tasks
  • Mobile workflow so carers can work from the field
  • Compliance-friendly reporting paths without locking the team into a heavyweight overseas SaaS stack
